Who we are?

Dishaa – The Platinum Hub.

Dishaa – The Platinum Hub is a renowned platinum jewellery wholesaler and manufacturer based in Mumbai, India.

Dishaa has expertise in creating creative designs and crafting exclusive platinum jewellery for jewellers across India and internationally. Innovation and quality have been the roots of our foundation and success.

Dishaa considers platinum a pure and precious partner in growth. Jewellery made with platinum is believed to be rare and eternal.

Dishaa has vast experience creating an exclusive range of platinum jewellery products. With inventory from top Indian manufacturers and superior customer service, it is one of the most preferred B2B platinum and gold jewellery manufacturers and wholesalers. It is trusted by 1000+ retail jewellers across India and internationally.

“Platinum is the perfect and precious metal for life. We go above and beyond every day to take it to every corner of the country.”
