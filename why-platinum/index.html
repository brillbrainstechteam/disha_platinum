Why platinum in your showroom?

Platinum is the most ductile of pure metals, more ductile than gold, silver, or copper. However, it is less malleable than gold. Platinum has excellent corrosion resistance, is stable at high temperatures, and has stable electrical properties. Gold and platinum are the two most commonly used metals for engagement and wedding rings. Both metals are unique yet similar in several ways. Let’s look at how gold and platinum compare in appearance, cost, durability, and comfort.

Platinum is a rare metal.

Its rareness makes it ideal for symbolising your most memorable and precious bonds of love, which you share only with your close ones. A celestial metal, platinum was found on Earth after a meteorite crash over 2 billion years ago. Platinum is 30 times rarer than gold and an ideal expression of rare love.

Reasons to choose platinum

Not just a metal. An emotion.

Platinum is a symbol of rarity and endurance. Unlike other metals, platinum’s strength, purity, and timeless appeal make it the ultimate choice for fine jewellery. Here’s why:

  • Purity & hypoallergenic nature – Platinum is 95% pure, perfect for sensitive skin.
  • Durability & strength – Highly resistant to wear and tear, ensuring heirloom-quality jewellery.
  • Naturally white – Unlike other metals, platinum does not change colour over time.
  • Rarity & exclusivity – 30 times rarer than gold, making it a precious possession.
Platinum for always

The forever metal.

Platinum is the ultimate metal to symbolise everlasting love and firm commitment. Its unique properties make it a “Forever Metal”:

  • Holds precious stones securely – A platinum setting provides unmatched security for diamonds and gemstones.
  • Symbol of rare love – Just like strong relationships, platinum never weakens over time.
Why platinum reigns supreme

Above all metals.

Platinum stands above all metals in jewellery, offering a combination of luxury, exclusivity, and durability.

  • Higher density – More substantial and luxurious feel compared to other metals.
  • Corrosion & tarnish resistant – Platinum doesn’t react with air or water, unlike gold or silver.
  • A legacy metal – Used by royalty and connoisseurs for centuries.
  • A symbol of status & prestige – Owning platinum is a statement of exclusivity.
